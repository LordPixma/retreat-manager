By Car
From M1 Junction 28, take A38 towards Derby. After ~2 miles, turn right onto B6016 (Sleet Moor Lane). The Hayes is signposted. Free parking available.
By Train
Nearest station: Alfreton (2 miles). Taxis available at the station. East Midlands Parkway also accessible (~20 mins by taxi).
