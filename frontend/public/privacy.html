Privacy Notice

Growth & Wisdom Family Retreat 2026 · Cloverleaf Christian Centre

This notice explains what personal information the Retreat Portal collects, why we use it, and the choices you have. We only use your information to run the retreat well and to keep you safe while you're with us.

What we collect

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone, postal address).
  • Date of birth (to organise age-appropriate activities and accommodation).
  • Dietary requirements, allergies, and any medical or accessibility needs you choose to share.
  • Emergency contact details.
  • Travel details (how you're arriving, and vehicle registration for parking).
  • Room and group allocation, activity-team membership, and payment status.
  • Anything you post on the community wall.

Why we use it

  • To register you, allocate rooms and groups, and plan catering and activities.
  • To keep you safe — medical, allergy, and emergency-contact information is used by the first-aid and organising team only.
  • To communicate retreat news and reminders.
  • To manage payments.

Who can see it

Your information is visible to the retreat's organising team (administrators). Medical and allergy details are restricted to those who need them for your care. Posts you make on the community wall are visible to other attendees. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for the retreat and for a reasonable period afterwards (for accounts, follow-up, and safeguarding), then delete or anonymise it.

Your choices

  • See your data: use “Download my data” on your My Details page for a full copy.
  • Correct it: update most details yourself on the My Details and Transport pages.
  • Delete it: ask the retreat team to remove your information.

Contact

For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, please contact the retreat organising team at Cloverleaf Christian Centre.

This is a plain-language summary provided by the retreat organisers; it is not legal advice.
